## Behavior notes

- **Menu items** of a kind row have no `on_click` (decision 31). `row_availability` is the gate, then `row_block`; the palette applies the same two and flips labels from the row (`Resume rollout`).
- **Roll back…** (menu) opens the drawer on the Overview and scrolls to the Revisions on the next paint (`DrawerState::reveal_revisions`, a `ScrollHandle` over the drawer sections). It is on whenever the gate and the row allow it (only a paused rollout or no earlier revision refuse): with the drawer closed the click opens it, which loads the ReplicaSets, and the section shows `Loading revisions…`. The sections are direct children of the scrolled drawer body, which is what lets `scroll_to_top_of_item` find the Revisions (a wrapper box around them left the request with nothing to scroll to). The palette entry `Roll back` opens the same way; once the revisions are loaded it becomes `Roll back to rev {n}`, a `PaletteTarget::RollBack` that carries the revision and starts the dialog itself (an argument-bearing item, decision 31).
- **Scale popover** is a floating card at the bottom centre (above the selection bar while rows are ticked), not the kit `Popover`, which needs a `Selectable` trigger. It closes on Escape, Cancel, a cursor move, a screen change, or the palette opening.
- **Palette `Ctrl ⏎`** turns the query into the replicas field (`PaletteArgument` key context). The kit `Dialog` confirms on Enter and would close the palette before the number is read, so `enter` is bound to `NoAction` there and handled once on a fresh press, like the confirm dialog.
- **Batch**: the dry-runs and commits run one item at a time through `checked_write`; every item must pass its dry-run; a failed commit does not stop the next item; a `Blocked` result stops the rest (`Not sent: {reason}`). One notice at the end (`Restart: 3 done, 1 failed (…)`). Warnings of a batch are summaries (`OnDelete` once, `Scaling down k of n`, `k of them are managed by an HPA`); Trigger now lists no concurrency lines in a batch.
- **Screens** (screenshot builds): `scale-popover`, `scale-confirm`, `restart-bulk-confirm`, and `<plural>-menu` (the drawer ⋯ menu open), all against the real first rows of the cluster.
- The palette reads `KindObject` flags (paused, suspended) of the cursor row for labels and reasons; it still reads no cell text.
- **Trigger now / Re-run texts** (UX walk M13): the dialog title reads `Trigger cronjob {name} now`; the confirm says `creates a Job` and `Create Job {prefix}… from {Kind} {name}` instead of the `metadata.generateName` field (the audit line keeps the field); the `created {job}` notice has a View button that reveals the new Job; a Resume confirm adds `Next run: {time} · in {n}`.
- **Set image… (UX round 3, O3):** `I` (menu, drawer ⋯, palette) on a Deployment, StatefulSet, or DaemonSet opens the value popover with a container picker (buttons, only when the template has several), the container's image with its tag selected, and a `Change cause` field. The confirm lists `image: old → new` and the stored cause (`WriteIntent.change_lines`), plus the Helm and paused-rollout warnings. The write is `SetContainerImage`: a strategic merge patch of the container (merged on `name`) and of the `kubernetes.io/change-cause` annotation of the workload, which its next ReplicaSet inherits; an empty cause sends `null`, so an earlier cause is cleared instead of naming the wrong change. It needs the `patch` right of the kind, like Restart. A Deployment's notice is the rollout-watched toast of Restart and Roll back (`watches_rollout`). The ticked rows play no part: one container of one workload. A container removed from the template between the popover and the patch would be added by the merge as a name-and-image stub: the shell reads the row again before it builds the intent and refuses a missing container.
- **Scale back (UX round 3, N5/N7/P12):** `ScaleWorkload` carries `previous`; the confirm reads `spec.replicas: 3 → 0`, the audit field has `from: "3"`, and the success toast (and the rollout-watch end toast that replaces it) holds a `Scale back to 3` button instead of View, which opens the same Scale confirm (`AppShell::scale_object`). The Scale popover selects the current value on open and when Tab reaches a number field, so typing `0` gives `0`, not `01`.
- **Autoscaler row (N18):** the Replicas section of a Deployment or StatefulSet drawer shows `Autoscaler web · 1–3 · at max` (HPAs from the Issues feed, `LiveContent::Autoscaler`): the name links to the HPA, and `Edit min / max…` opens the range popover over the drawer (gated by `EditHpaRange`).
- **Scale-down side effects (UX round 3, P20):** the Scale confirm and popover add, for a StatefulSet with claim templates, `Keeps the PersistentVolumeClaims of the removed pods (data-catalog-db-1)` (`Deletes` when the retention policy deletes on scale), and for each PDB of the namespace that selects the workload, `PDB catalog-db minAvailable 2: evictions will be blocked at 1 replica` (`scale_effects.rs`; counts and percentages round up as the budget controller does; scaling to 0 names none). The PDBs come from the Issues feed; the workload's own selector stands for its pod labels.
